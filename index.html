03 · FOLLOW THE RESEARCH LOGIC

From the archaeological question to the evaluation.

The central problem is simple: what were Breadloaf Idols used for? Because there is no known ground-truth answer, the work evaluates generated claims through novelty, diversity, and evidence-based plausibility.

A

OVERALL LOGIC

What exactly is being compared?

Human and AI-generated claims are compared in two ways. First, their idea space is evaluated for novelty and diversity. Second, each claim is compared with facts about the idols to estimate plausibility.

B

KEY DISTINCTION

Relevant facts are not the same as explained facts.

A fact can be relevant to judging a claim without being explained by that claim. The example below shows the distinction used in the plausibility measures.

RR · Which facts matter? EC · Which relevant facts are explained? OEC · How much of all available evidence is explained?
C

EXPERIMENT 1

Human vs AI ideation

Human and AI claims are organized into a shared category space, then compared to examine how broadly and differently the two sources generate possible explanations.

D

EXPERIMENT 2

Measuring claim explanatory power

AI-generated claims are compared against atomic archaeological facts. Relevant facts are identified first, then the evaluation asks which of those facts the claim actually explains.
